domesticated
Ceasing to be a parasite is meant to be a way to persist — but not at these defaults. The bonus a domesticated copy earns (wDom) ships at 0.01, under the 0.03–0.075 persistence band measured on guard 8's arm (beta 0.1, pDom 0.2, N 200, gen 600) — the band belongs to that arm, not to the model. So domesticated copies appear, peak, and are lost to zero at every seed. The domestication probability slider changes how often the event happens, not whether it sticks; there is no control for the bonus. A calibration limit, not a missing mechanism, left alone on purpose: every registered result was measured at it.
